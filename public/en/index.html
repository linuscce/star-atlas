Starry Sky — Step by Step

A textbook of the night sky for astronomy olympiad preparation: 53 lessons and all 88 constellations, one at a time. Each new constellation is found from ones you already know by following yellow pointers, starting from the Big Dipper.

Start with the first lesson
Where do you watch the sky from?

In the southern hemisphere the lessons follow their own route: from the Southern Cross and Centaurus to Orion and Scorpius, with the northern constellations at the end.

Lessons

A map with the new constellation, how to find it, its main stars and objects, olympiad facts and a self-test.

Practice

Four kinds of questions: recognize a constellation, show it on the map, find and name a star. Mistakes come up more often.

Problems

Olympiad-style sky problems: maps without lines, star visibility, the Sun and the Moon. Answers are checked at once, with solutions.

Atlas

The whole sky with search. Tap a star to see its coordinates and visibility at your latitude.

Reference

The Greek alphabet, the brightest stars, the Sun in the constellations, visibility formulas, all 88 constellations.
