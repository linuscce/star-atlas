Eridanus constellation — how to find it in the sky

Eridanus · River, gen. Eridani · Eri

Open the lesson on the interactive map

How to find it

Next to Rigel (~3.5° to the west) is Cursa (β Eridani), the source of the river. From here Eridanus meanders west, then far south — down to Achernar (0.5m), “the end of the river”.

What it looks like

The river is a long winding chain of 3–4m stars. Only its very end is bright — Achernar.

Main stars

  • 2.8mCursa β Eridani
  • 3.0mZaurak γ Eridani
  • 2.9mAcamar θ¹ Eridani
  • 0.4mAchernar α Eridani
  • 3.7mε Eridani

Good to know

  • Eridanus is the 6th largest constellation and stretches almost 60° in declination.
  • Achernar cannot be seen from mid-northern latitudes: its δ = −57°, so it rises only south of latitude 33° N.
  • ε Eridani is one of the nearest Sun-like stars (10.5 light-years).
  • Achernar spins so fast that it is strongly flattened — one of the flattest stars ever studied.
