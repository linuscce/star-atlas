How to find it

Between Centaurus and Scorpius are five small constellations. Lupus, the Wolf, is between Centaurus and Scorpius. Circinus is tiny, next to α Centauri. Triangulum Australe is a bright triangle (Atria, 1.9m) east of α Centauri. Norma is dim, between Lupus and Triangulum Australe. Ara, the Altar, is below the tail of Scorpius.

What it looks like

The most noticeable are Triangulum Australe (almost equilateral) and Ara (two pairs of bright stars).

Main stars

  • 2.3mα Lupi
  • 2.7mβ Lupi

Good to know

  • Lupus and Ara are ancient constellations of Ptolemy. Triangulum Australe was invented by the Dutch navigators Keyser and de Houtman; Circinus and Norma by Lacaille.
  • The Milky Way runs through Norma and Ara.
  • “Atria” is short for Alpha Trianguli Australis.
  • The northern part of Lupus rises above the horizon at latitudes around 45° N.
