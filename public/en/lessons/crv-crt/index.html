Corvus and Crater — how to find them in the sky

Corvus · Crow, gen. Corvi · Crv
Crater · Cup, gen. Crateris · Crt

Open the lesson on the interactive map

How to find it

Southwest of Spica (to the right and below) is a noticeable quadrilateral of 2.5–3m stars: Corvus, the Crow. Farther west is the dim Crater, the Cup, which looks like a goblet.

What it looks like

Corvus is a “sail” or a lopsided square. Crater is a semicircular cup on a stem. Both sit on Hydra’s back.

Main stars

  • 2.6mGienah γ Corvi
  • 2.7mKraz β Corvi
  • 2.9mAlgorab δ Corvi
  • 4.0mAlchiba α Corvi
  • 4.1mAlkes α Crateris

Good to know

  • In Corvus the star α (Alchiba, 4m) is one of the faintest in the figure: the letters were not given out by brightness.
  • Myth: Apollo sent the crow to fetch water in a cup; the crow dawdled and lied, blaming a water snake (Hydra). Its punishment: it sits next to the Cup but can never drink.
  • The colliding “Antennae” galaxies are in Corvus.
