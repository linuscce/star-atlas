Piscis Austrinus constellation — how to find it in the sky

Piscis Austrinus · Southern Fish, gen. Piscis Austrini · PsA

Open the lesson on the interactive map

How to find it

Extend the western side of the Square of Pegasus (Scheat → Markab) to the south about 3.5 times — a lone bright star low above the horizon: Fomalhaut.

What it looks like

Fomalhaut is almost the whole constellation: the other stars are faint and form a small ring — the body of the fish.

Main stars

  • 1.2mFomalhaut α Piscis Austrini
  • 4.2mε Piscis Austrini

Good to know

  • Fomalhaut (“mouth of the fish”) is the lone bright star of the southern part of the autumn sky. At 56° N it rises only ~5°.
  • Around Fomalhaut is a dust disk imaged by the Hubble and Webb telescopes.
  • The Southern Fish drinks the water poured by Aquarius.
