Cygnus constellation — how to find it in the sky

Cygnus · Swan, gen. Cygni · Cyg

Open the lesson on the interactive map

How to find it

From Vega to the east (left), ~24° — Deneb, the tail of the Swan and the second corner of the Summer Triangle.

What it looks like

A large cross — the “Northern Cross”. Deneb is at the top (the tail), Albireo at the foot (the beak), the crossbar is the wings, and Sadr is in the center. The Swan flies south along the Milky Way.

Main stars

  • 1.3mDeneb α Cygni
  • 3.0mAlbireo β¹ Cygni
  • 2.2mSadr γ Cygni
  • 2.5mAljanah ε Cygni
  • 2.9mδ Cygni
  • 5.2m61 Cygni 61 Cygni

Interesting objects

  • M39 (open cluster)
  • North America Nebula (NGC 7000)

Good to know

  • Deneb is a distant (~2000 light-years) supergiant, tens of thousands of times more luminous than the Sun.
  • Albireo is the most beautiful colored double: a golden and a blue star (visible in a small telescope).
  • 61 Cygni was the first star whose distance was measured (Bessel, 1838).
  • Cygnus X-1, the first reliable black hole candidate, is in Cygnus.
  • Next to Deneb is the North America Nebula.
