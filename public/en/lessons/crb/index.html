Corona Borealis constellation — how to find it in the sky

Corona Borealis · Northern Crown, gen. Coronae Borealis · CrB

Open the lesson on the interactive map

How to find it

East of (to the left of) Arcturus, about 20° away, is a small semicircle of seven stars. The brightest is Alphecca, also called Gemma, “the jewel in the crown”.

What it looks like

A neat arc open to the north. Very noticeable despite the modest brightness of its stars.

Main stars

  • 2.2mAlphecca α Coronae Borealis

Good to know

  • T Coronae Borealis is a recurrent nova: it flared up in 1866 and 1946 (about once in 80 years) and for a few days became visible to the naked eye. The next outburst was expected in the mid-2020s.
  • Alphecca is an eclipsing variable star.
  • Myth: this is the crown of Ariadne, which Dionysus threw into the sky.
