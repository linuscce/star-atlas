Scorpius constellation — how to find it in the sky

Scorpius · Scorpion, gen. Scorpii · Sco

Open the lesson on the interactive map

How to find it

Regulus, Spica and Antares lie almost on one line — the ecliptic. Extend Regulus → Spica another ~46° — the red Antares, “the heart of the Scorpion”.

What it looks like

The head is a vertical arc of three stars (Dschubba, Acrab and π), then the body with Antares, and a long curved tail with a stinger (Shaula and Lesath).

Interesting objects

  • M4 (globular cluster near Antares)
  • M6 Butterfly Butterfly Cluster
  • M7 Ptolemy Ptolemy Cluster

Good to know

  • “Antares” means “rival of Mars”: a red supergiant similar in color to Mars.
  • At 56° N Antares rises only ~8° above the horizon, and the tail of the Scorpion never rises at all.
  • The Sun spends only ~7 days in Scorpius (November 23–29).
  • Myth: the Scorpion stung Orion, so they are on opposite sides of the sky. At mid-latitudes they are never seen at the same time.
