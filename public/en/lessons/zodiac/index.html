The ecliptic and the zodiac

13 constellations on the ecliptic

The Sun passes through 13 constellations — the 12 of the “zodiac” and Ophiuchus. In order (from west to east, the way the Sun moves): Pisces → Aries → Taurus → Gemini → Cancer → Leo → Virgo → Libra → Scorpius → Ophiuchus → Sagittarius → Capricornus → Aquarius.

These are not the astrological “signs”: because of precession and the unequal sizes of the constellations, the dates differ a lot. The Sun spends less than a week in Scorpius and about 45 days in Virgo.

How to use this in problems

  • The constellation the Sun is in right now is not visible (it is in the daytime sky).
  • The constellation opposite the Sun (12h away in right ascension) is visible all night; at midnight it is in the south.
  • The full Moon is always opposite the Sun: in December it is in Gemini or Taurus, in June in Sagittarius or Ophiuchus.
  • The Moon and planets are always close to the ecliptic (within a few degrees).

Special points

  • Vernal equinox (α = 0h) — in Pisces.
  • Summer solstice (α = 6h) — in Taurus, right at the border with Gemini (until 1990 it was in Gemini).
  • Autumnal equinox (α = 12h) — in Virgo.
  • Winter solstice (α = 18h) — in Sagittarius.
