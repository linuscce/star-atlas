Pisces constellation — how to find it in the sky

Pisces · Fishes, gen. Piscium · Psc

Open the lesson on the interactive map

How to find it

Below the Great Square of Pegasus (south of the side Markab — Algenib) is the “Circlet”: a ring of 5–7 faint stars. This is the western fish. From the Circlet a long “ribbon” runs east to the star Alrescha (the knot), and from there north, under Andromeda, to the second fish.

What it looks like

A huge letter V hugging the Square of Pegasus from below and from the left; Alrescha is at the point of the V.

Main stars

  • 3.8mAlrescha α Piscium
  • 3.6mη Piscium
  • 3.7mγ Piscium
  • 4.0mω Piscium

Good to know

  • The vernal equinox point is now in Pisces (α = 0h, δ = 0°), about 7° south of ω Piscium.
  • The Sun is in Pisces from about March 12 to April 18, including the day of the vernal equinox.
  • The brightest star of Pisces is η (3.6m), not α.
