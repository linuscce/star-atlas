Equuleus and Lacerta — how to find them in the sky

Equuleus · Little Horse, gen. Equulei · Equ
Lacerta · Lizard, gen. Lacertae · Lac

Open the lesson on the interactive map

How to find it

Equuleus is a small trapezoid of faint stars between Enif and Delphinus. Lacerta is a dim zigzag between Deneb and Cassiopeia, below Cepheus.

What it looks like

Both constellations are “patches”: they are remembered by their neighbors, not by their shape.

Main stars

  • 3.9mKitalpha α Equulei
  • 3.8mα Lacertae

Good to know

  • Equuleus is the 2nd smallest constellation (only Crux is smaller).
  • Lacerta was introduced by Hevelius (1687).
  • BL Lacertae was first taken for a variable star, but it turned out to be the core of a distant active galaxy — the prototype of blazars.
