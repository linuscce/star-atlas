Andromeda constellation — how to find it in the sky

Andromeda · Chained Princess, gen. Andromedae · And

Open the lesson on the interactive map

How to find it

From Alpheratz (the corner of the square) a chain of bright stars runs northeast: Mirach and Almach. From Mirach go up (north) through two faint stars, μ and ν — next to ν is a hazy spot: the Andromeda Galaxy, M31.

What it looks like

Two chains of stars diverging from Alpheratz — like the letter V lying on its side.

Good to know

  • M31 is the most distant object easily seen with the naked eye: ~2.5 million light-years.
  • M31 and the Milky Way are approaching each other and will collide in ~4–5 billion years.
  • Almach (γ) is a beautiful double: an orange and a blue star.
  • Myth: Andromeda, the daughter of Cepheus and Cassiopeia, was chained to a rock as a sacrifice to the sea monster Cetus; Perseus rescued her.
