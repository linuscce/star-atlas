Triangulum and Aries — how to find them in the sky

Triangulum · Triangle, gen. Trianguli · Tri
Aries · Ram, gen. Arietis · Ari

Open the lesson on the interactive map

How to find it

Below Andromeda (south of Mirach) is a small narrow triangle of 3–4m stars: Triangulum. Lower still is Aries: a short curved line of three stars; the brightest is the orange Hamal (2m).

What it looks like

Triangulum is an elongated “wedge”. Aries is a “hockey stick”: Hamal, Sheratan, Mesarthim.

Main stars

  • 3.4mMothallah α Trianguli
  • 3.0mβ Trianguli
  • 2.0mHamal α Arietis
  • 2.6mSheratan β Arietis
  • 3.9mMesarthim γ¹ Arietis

Interesting objects

  • M33 Triangulum Galaxy

Good to know

  • M33 is the Triangulum Galaxy (~3 million light-years), the third largest in the Local Group after M31 and the Milky Way.
  • The vernal equinox point, from which right ascension is counted, is called the “First Point of Aries”. But because of precession it has been in Pisces for ~2000 years.
  • The Sun is in Aries from about April 18 to May 14.
  • Mesarthim (γ Arietis) was one of the first double stars discovered with a telescope (Hooke, 1664).
