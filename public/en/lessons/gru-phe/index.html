How to find it

Below Fomalhaut (~20° south) is Grus, the Crane, with the bright Alnair (1.7m) and a long neck made of a chain of stars. To the east, on the way to Achernar, is Phoenix (Ankaa). Between Fomalhaut and Cetus are the dim Sculptor and Fornax (by the river Eridanus). Tucana is closer to the pole; the Small Magellanic Cloud is in it.

What it looks like

Grus is a long diagonal chain of stars. The rest are faint areas around the south galactic pole.

Good to know

  • The south galactic pole is in Sculptor.
  • The “Hubble Ultra Deep Field” (2004) is an image of a tiny patch of sky in Fornax: it shows ~10,000 galaxies.
  • 47 Tucanae is the second brightest globular cluster in the sky after ω Centauri.
  • β Gruis is a red giant next to blue-white Alnair: a striking color contrast.
