Sagittarius constellation — how to find it in the sky

Sagittarius · Archer, gen. Sagittarii · Sgr

Open the lesson on the interactive map

How to find it

Extend the line Deneb → Altair by the same distance again — you will land in Sagittarius (on the star Nunki). Or look to the left (east) of the Scorpion’s tail for the characteristic “Teapot” of eight stars.

What it looks like

The Teapot: the spout points west, toward the Scorpion’s tail, the handle east. “Steam” rises from the spout — the brightest cloud of the Milky Way.

Main stars

  • 1.8mKaus Australis ε Sagittarii
  • 2.0mNunki σ Sagittarii
  • 2.8mKaus Borealis λ Sagittarii
  • 2.7mKaus Media δ Sagittarii
  • 2.6mAscella ζ Sagittarii
  • 4.0mRukbat α Sagittarii

Interesting objects

  • Galactic Center (Sagittarius A*)
  • M8 Lagoon Lagoon Nebula
  • M20 Trifid Nebula
  • M22 (globular cluster)
  • M17 Omega Nebula

Good to know

  • In the direction of Sagittarius lies the center of our Galaxy (~26,000 light-years), with the supermassive black hole Sagittarius A*.
  • The winter solstice point is in Sagittarius: on December 21–22 the Sun is here, at the southernmost point of the ecliptic.
  • α Sagittarii (Rukbat) is only 4m and far from the Teapot.
  • At 56° N, Kaus Australis (δ = −34°) barely touches the horizon, so the whole Teapot is seen only farther south.
  • There are many nebulae and clusters here: the Lagoon (M8), the Trifid (M20), the Omega (M17) and the globular cluster M22.
